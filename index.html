I use mathematical modelling to describe growth of tumour influenced by cellular metabolism and extracellular remodelling operated by the cell. In the future, I would like to developp and implement my own workflows and method to unravel the cellular dynamic and heterogeneity.
Agent-Based Modelling of glioblastoma cell growth
While cells rely mainly on oxidative processes to generate their energy, cancer cells have been to have increased usage of glycolysis leading to increased glucose consumption and lower pH. This behaviour has been observed in normoxia and was called the Warburg effect. Through mathematical modelling of tumour growth, I showed that both oxygen fluctuations and genetic regulation may trigger the appearance of a Warburg phenotype in model.

Statistical data analysis of bulk and single-cell transcriptomic data
Transcriptomic data are widely used to detect differences between gene expression pattern between two factors. This standard analysis has a wide vareiety of application including finding targets for disease treatment or merely assessing cell-types in a cell population. I applied differential expression analysis and pathway enrichment to determine the frequently deregulated pathways in glioblastoma bulk RNA data. Using methods for single-cell, such as Seurat and scanpy, I assessed the batch effect in data with the goal of correcting this biais and searched for markers to identify cell-types in different cells clusters. spinicck/spinicck.github.io
